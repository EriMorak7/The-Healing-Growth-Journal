### 2. How to Post Weekly Sunday Love Letters

Every Sunday morning, follow these steps to add your weekly letter or poem:

1. Log into the Editorial Desk and click **"Write New Piece"** in the top bar.
2. **Enter Title & Excerpt:**
   * Example Title: *The Sunday Love Series: To Hold Love Tenderly, Even When It Must Rest*
   * Example Excerpt: *A tender reflection on allowing love to breathe without the pressure of performance.*
3. **Toggle Sunday Love Series:**
   * In the right-hand sidebar under **Series & Placement**, check **"The Sunday Love Series"**.
   * *(This automatically applies the sage green "Sunday Love" badge and routes the letter into `/sunday-love-series`.)*
4. **Attach a Cover Image:**
   * Click **"Click to Upload Photo"** to upload your cover artwork or photograph.
5. **Format Your Letter:**
   * Use the toolbar buttons for italicized poems, reflective quotes, or section dividers.
6. **Preview Before Publishing:**
   * Click the **"Live Reader Preview"** tab at the top of the editor. This shows you exactly how your readers will see the piece with editorial drop caps and typography.
7. Click **"Publish Piece"**.

---

### 3. Publishing Essays in the Four Pillars

For standard essays and therapeutic reflections:
1. Select one or more relevant categories under **"What We Write About (Pillars)"**:
   * **Emotional Healing**
   * **Personal Growth**
   * **Healthy Relationships**
   * **Quiet Reflection**
2. Add comma-separated tags (e.g., `grief, heartbreak, self-discovery, boundaries`).
3. Set your reading time (e.g., `4 min read`).
4. To highlight the piece prominently at the top of your homepage, check **"Featured on Homepage"**.

---

### 4. Using the Writing Studio & Formatting Toolbar

The writing studio includes quick formatting tools:
* **Bold (`**text**`)**: Highlight key thoughts.
* **Italic (`*text*`)**: For gentle emphasis, book titles, or poetic verse.
* **Heading 2 (`## Section`)**: Create distinct sections.
* **Heading 3 (`### Subsection`)**: For smaller subtopics.
* **Reflective Quote (`> "Text"`)**: Formats text into an editorial blockquote with a dark forest green vertical border and warm background.
* **Bullet Points (`- Item`)**: Perfect for guided questions or reflection steps.
* **Divider Line (`---`)**: Adds a clean separator between sections.
* **Insert Story Photo**: Click *"Insert Story Photo"* to upload and position high-resolution illustrations directly inside your article body.

---

### 5. Managing Digital Workbooks & Shop Products

1. In the sidebar, navigate to **"Digital Products"**.
2. Click **"+ Add New Product"**.
3. **Fill in Product Details:**
   * **Product Name**: e.g., *30-Day Gentle Grief Recovery Workbook*
   * **Price in NGN**: Enter the numeric price (e.g., `5000` for `₦5,000`).
   * **Description**: Explain the therapeutic purpose and journey of the workbook.
   * **Who It's For**: Clarify the intended reader (e.g., *Anyone navigating recent loss, heartbreak, or transition*).
   * **What's Included**: List what the buyer receives (e.g., *Printable 60-page PDF, 30 daily guided prompts, weekly grounding audio link*).
   * **Mockup Image**: Upload a cover preview image.
4. Set **Published in Shop** to active and click **"Save Product"**.
5. Once saved, customers can instantly purchase the guide via Paystack and download their PDF directly.

---

### 6. Reading & Replying to Contact Inquiries

When readers submit letters or questions through the `/contact` form:
1. Navigate to **"Contact Inquiries"** in the sidebar.
2. New messages appear with an orange **"New"** badge.
3. Click **"View"** to read the full inquiry in a reading modal.
4. Click **"Reply via Email"** to open your email client pre-populated with the reader's address and subject line.
5. You can toggle messages between Read/Unread or delete resolved inquiries.

### 7. Connecting Website & Substack

* **Newsletter Engine:** All *"Join on Substack"* and *"Subscribe"* buttons connect directly to `https://thehealingandgrowthjournal.substack.com`.
* **Syndication (RSS):** Your website publishes a live RSS feed at `/feed.xml` for readers using RSS readers and feed aggregators.
* **SEO Library:** The 10 evergreen prompt pages (`/prompts`) continuously attract new readers searching on Google for grief, breakup, and self-discovery prompts, directing them into your Substack subscriber base.
