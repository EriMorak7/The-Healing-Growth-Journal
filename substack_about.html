Welcome.

Maybe you came here because you are grieving someone you love.

Maybe something ended, and you are still trying to understand how to begin again.

Maybe you are navigating heartbreak, disappointment, a difficult transition, or simply the quiet confusion of becoming someone you have never been before.

Or maybe you are doing okay, but you know there is still a part of yourself you want to understand more deeply.

Whatever brought you here, I hope you stay a while.

Welcome to The Healing and Growth Journal.

A place for healing, reflection, growth, and becoming.

This Journal was born from grief.

Hi, I’m Glory.

I’m a counselling psychologist, teacher, and writer, and the person behind this Journal.

On March 19, 2025, I lost my father, just two days before my convocation.

I had imagined that season differently. There should have been celebration, photographs, family, laughter, and the joy of reaching a milestone I had worked so hard for.

Instead, grief arrived.

And grief changed me.

I began to understand that healing is rarely as straightforward as we imagine. Sometimes you think you have healed until a voice, a memory, a familiar place, or someone else’s story touches a wound you thought had already closed.

That happened to me when a friend sent me a voice note crying about the loss of her own father.

I found myself crying too.

Not only for her, but for the parts of my own grief I had not finished meeting.

That moment reminded me:

Healing is not always about moving on. Sometimes it is about learning how to carry what changed you.

And somewhere in that journey, The Healing and Growth Journal was born.

But this Journal is about more than grief.

Because we don’t only heal from losing people.

We heal from relationships that ended.

From friendships that changed.

From dreams that didn’t happen.

From disappointment.

From rejection.

From versions of ourselves we have outgrown.

From the pressure to have everything figured out.

From becoming successful and still wondering why we don’t feel fulfilled.

From giving so much of ourselves to others that we eventually have to ask:

Who am I when I stop trying to be everything for everyone?

This is where the Journal lives.

In the space between who you were, who you are, and who you are becoming.

What you’ll find here

Through personal essays, stories, letters, reflections, poems, journal prompts, and thoughtful conversations, we explore:

Healing
Grief, loss, emotional healing, disappointment, letting go, self-compassion, and finding peace.

Growth
Self-awareness, identity, confidence, purpose, personal development, and becoming.

Relationships
Love, friendship, boundaries, heartbreak, connection, and the complicated ways people shape us.

Reflection
The quiet observations, questions, experiences, and lessons that help us understand ourselves a little better.

This isn’t a space where I will tell you to simply “stay positive.”

I believe there are moments when you need encouragement.

Moments when you need truth.

Moments when you don’t need permission to admit that you are not okay.

And moments when you simply need someone to say:

“I understand. You don’t have to carry this alone.”

Who is behind the Journal?

I’m not here as someone who has figured life out.

I’m learning too.

My professional background in counselling psychology informs the way I think about healing and human behaviour, while my own experiences remind me that no textbook can fully explain what it means to lose, love, change, and begin again.

So I write from both understanding and experience.

But I don’t want this space to feel clinical.

I want it to feel human.

Like sitting with someone who isn’t rushing you to get better.

Someone willing to sit with you in the difficult parts while still believing that your story can become something beautiful.

Because sometimes the right words don’t fix the pain.

But they can help us name it.

They can help us feel seen.

And sometimes, they can give us enough courage to take the next step.
